Design System

The foundations and components behind the bookstore: teal for trust and knowledge, fresh green as the secondary color, and golden yellow reserved for the actions that matter, on a soft off-white background. Every value lives as a CSS custom property in assets/css/styles.css, and every page works in English (left-to-right) and Persian (right-to-left).

Color

Teal #0F7C82 is the primary color for buttons, links and dark surfaces. Green #68A936 is secondary, used for success, “new” badges and highlights. Yellow #F4B323 is the accent for high-intent actions (Buy now, Checkout) and always carries dark text for contrast. The page background is white, alternate sections use a light tint, and body text is #263238. ★ marks the brand values. Use the palette button in the corner to try other color templates; every shade is derived from three brand colors.

Typography

English uses Fraunces (display serif) for headings and Inter for body and UI. Persian switches both to Vazirmatn, with a taller line-height and no letter-spacing so letters join correctly. Headings scale fluidly with clamp().

Cards & book covers

The book card is the core shopping unit: cover on a soft stage, badges in the top corner, a wishlist heart, quick view on hover, and one-tap add to cart. Covers tilt and lift on hover so they feel tactile. In Persian the spine moves to the right, like a real Persian book.

Languages & right-to-left

The store runs in Persian and English. The switch in the header remembers your choice. Persian flips the whole layout right-to-left, using CSS logical properties so spacing, borders and icons mirror automatically.

Direction<html dir="rtl"> for Persian, "ltr" for English
Numbers & datesPersian digits and the Solar Hijri calendar via Intl
IconsArrows and chevrons mirror; stars fill from the right
ContentTitles, descriptions, authors and reviews are translated

UX principles

How the system turns a premium bookstore feel into fast discovery and effortless buying.

Discovery firstSearch is always one tap away; categories, tabs and AI picks surface books in two clicks or fewer.
Minimal checkout3 steps, digital-only orders skip shipping, guest checkout, wallet payments.
One accent per viewYellow marks the single highest-intent action so it is unmistakable.
Touch friendly44px minimum targets, swipeable carousels, a sticky buy bar on mobile.
AccessibleSemantic landmarks, skip link, labelled controls, visible focus, reduced-motion support.
Trust signalsRatings, verified reviews, clear pricing, returns and secure-payment cues near every CTA.
