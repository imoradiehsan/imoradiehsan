Design System

The foundations and components behind the bookstore: a deep-navy palette for trust, warm cream for the bookshop feel, and gold accents reserved for the actions that matter. Every value lives as a CSS custom property in assets/css/styles.css.

Color

Navy communicates trust and knowledge and carries text, primary buttons and dark surfaces. Cream warms section backgrounds and product stages. Gold is the accent for high-intent actions (Buy now, Checkout, Sale) — use it sparingly so it keeps its pull. All text pairs meet WCAG AA contrast.

Typography

Fraunces (display serif) gives headings a literary, editorial voice. Inter handles body and UI for maximum legibility. Headings scale fluidly with clamp(); body text stays at 16px with a 1.6 line-height for comfortable reading.

Cards & book covers

The book card is the core commerce unit: cover on a cream stage, badges top-left, wishlist top-right, quick-view on hover, and a one-tap add-to-cart. Covers tilt and lift on hover to feel tactile.

UX principles

How the system turns a premium bookstore atmosphere into fast discovery and effortless buying.

Discovery firstSearch is always one tap away; categories, tabs and AI picks surface books in ≤ 2 clicks.
Minimal checkout3 steps, digital-only orders skip shipping, guest checkout, wallet payments.
One accent per viewGold marks the single highest-intent action so CTAs are unmistakable.
Touch friendly44px minimum targets, swipeable carousels with scroll-snap, sticky mobile buy bar.
AccessibleSemantic landmarks, skip link, labelled controls, visible focus, reduced-motion support.
Trust signalsRatings, verified reviews, clear pricing, returns and secure-payment cues near every CTA.
